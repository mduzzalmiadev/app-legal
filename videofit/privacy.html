Privacy Policy

Resize Video · Last updated 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Resize Video ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. We built Resize Video to work almost entirely on your device, and we collect as little as possible.

Summary

  • We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your videos are processed on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any server.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.

Photos and Videos

When you choose a video to resize, the App accesses only the item you select through the system photo picker. All resizing, reframing, background fill, and export happens locally on your device using Apple frameworks. Exported videos are saved back to your Photo Library only when you ask the App to save or share them. We have no access to your Photo Library and no copy of your media ever leaves your device through the App.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Resize Video offers an optional paid subscription ("Pro"). Payments are processed by Apple through your App Store account; we never see your card or billing details.

To manage entitlements, unlock Pro features, and support "Restore Purchases", we use RevenueCat as our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user identifier, your purchase and receipt information from Apple, and basic device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version, and country). This data is used only to deliver and restore your subscription. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

Information Apple May Provide

If you have enabled sharing of diagnostics and usage data in your device settings, Apple may share aggregated, non-identifying App Store analytics with us (for example, download counts and crash statistics). This is controlled by your Apple settings, not by the App.

Data Retention

Because the App does not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to retain. Subscription records held by RevenueCat and Apple are retained by those providers according to their own policies and for as long as needed to operate the subscription.

Children

Resize Video is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to its processing (for example under the GDPR or CCPA). Since we do not hold identifiable data about you, most requests relate to subscription records held by our providers. Contact us at the address below and we will help you exercise your rights or direct your request to the appropriate provider.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through an in-App notice.
