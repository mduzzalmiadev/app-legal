Privacy Policy

Blur Editor · Last updated: October 14, 2026

The short version

  • Your photos and videos are edited entirely on your device. We never upload, see or store them.
  • We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name or email address.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking in the app.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple and our subscription service, RevenueCat, using an anonymous ID.

This Privacy Policy explains how Blur Editor (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad. By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

1. Your photos and videos

Blur Editor is built to protect privacy, so your media stays with you:

  • On-device processing. Blurring, pixelation, effects, filters, face detection, license plate and text detection, person (background) detection and object tracking all run on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Your photos and videos are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Choosing media. Photos and videos are picked with Apple's system photo picker. The app only receives the items you select; it can't browse the rest of your library.
  • Saving. When you save an edit, the app asks for permission to add items to your Photos library. It saves a new copy; your original is never changed or deleted.
  • Projects. If you save a project, a private copy of the photo or video and your edits are stored in the app's own storage on your device so you can keep editing later. Deleting the project or the app removes them.
  • Export files. Exported files are prepared in a temporary folder on your device. You can clear it at any time in Settings → Clear Cache.
  • Hidden metadata. Exported photos don't include location or camera metadata. For videos, the “Remove Hidden Metadata” option (on by default) strips location, device and date information from the exported file.

2. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy

You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app. The app keeps working without them, except for the feature that needs them.

3. Information we collect

Subscription and purchase information

If you view plans or subscribe to Blur Editor Pro, payment is processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your payment card details.

To check whether you have an active subscription and to let you restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (not linked to your name or email);
  • your purchase and subscription history for this app (product, price, dates, renewal status) as provided by the App Store;
  • basic technical information needed to deliver the service, such as app version, device model, operating system version, country or region, and IP address.

This information is used only to provide and manage your subscription, prevent fraud, and keep the service working. It is not used for advertising.

Support emails

If you contact us through Settings → Contact Support, we receive the email address you send from and what you write. The email is pre-filled with the app version, your device model and iOS version to help us fix problems; you can delete that before sending. We use it only to answer you.

What we don't collect

  • No photos, videos, or anything you edit.
  • No account, name or contact information (unless you email us).
  • No precise location.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services from us.

Ratings

After some exports, the app may ask you to rate it using Apple's rating prompt or a link to the App Store. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

4. How information is shared

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. Information is shared only:

  • with Apple, which processes payments and the App Store;
  • with RevenueCat, our subscription service provider, as described above;
  • if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or others.

5. Data retention

Media, projects and settings stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app. Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and accounting obligations. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

6. Security

Because editing happens on your device, your media never leaves it through the app. Data exchanged with Apple and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the limited information we handle.

7. Your rights and choices

  • You can delete projects in the app, clear the export cache in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. To make a request, email us at the address below. Because subscription data uses an anonymous ID, we may ask for details (such as a purchase receipt) to find your records.

8. Children

Blur Editor is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Subscription information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the “Last updated” date above. If changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app or on this page.
