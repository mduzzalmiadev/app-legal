Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Expense Tracker ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App.

Summary: Expense Tracker is built to keep your financial data on your device. We don't require an account, we don't run ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The only outside service involved is RevenueCat, which manages App Store subscription purchases.

1. Information You Provide

The App lets you record expenses, categories, budgets, and a preferred currency. This information is entered directly by you and is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). It is not uploaded to our servers, because we don't operate any.

2. Siri, Shortcuts & Back Tap

If you use Siri, the Shortcuts app, or Back Tap to log an expense, that action is handled by Apple's App Intents framework and writes to the same on-device storage the App uses. This data does not leave your device as part of that process.

3. Exporting Your Data

You may export your expenses as a CSV file using the system share sheet. Where that file is sent — Mail, Files, Messages, a cloud drive, etc. — is entirely your choice, and we have no visibility into or access to it.

4. Subscriptions & RevenueCat

Expense Tracker Pro is offered as an in-app purchase/subscription, processed by Apple and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat. To validate and manage purchases, RevenueCat may process:

  • An anonymous, app-specific identifier for your device/installation
  • Purchase and subscription status (e.g., active plan, renewal date, receipt data from Apple)

This information is used only to unlock Pro features and is not linked to your expense data. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for details on how they process this data.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account creation, email, or personal profile is required to use the App
  • No advertising identifiers and no ad networks
  • No third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDKs
  • No location data

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your expenses, categories, and budgets remain on your device until you delete them in the App or delete the App itself, which removes all locally stored data. Since we don't hold a copy of your data on any server, we're unable to retrieve or delete it on your behalf after that point.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. The "Effective date" above will be updated whenever changes are made.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at mduzzalmia.dev@gmail.com.
