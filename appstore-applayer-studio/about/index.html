About AppLayer Studio

AppLayer Studio is an independent studio building apps for Apple platforms. One developer designs, builds and maintains every app, one focused tool at a time.

What we make

There are currently 23 apps on the App Store across 8 categories: audio editing and ringtones, PDF scanning and conversion, dual-camera and screen recording, coloring and drawing, productivity and writing tools, and everyday device utilities. 2 of them also have native Mac versions. Browse them all in the app directory.

The apps are published on the App Store under the developer account MD UZZAL MIA, which is the seller name you'll see on each listing. You can also view the full list on the App Store developer page.

How the apps work

Most apps are free to download and offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions for advanced features. Prices vary by country and are always shown on the App Store and inside the app before you pay. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple, so you can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings at any time.

Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its page on this site and from its App Store listing. Several apps process files on your device. Where a listing says so, we mention it on the app's page.

How we write guides

Our guides follow a few rules:

  • Answer the question first, then explain the steps.
  • Include Apple's free built-in options where they exist, even when one of our apps is an alternative.
  • Only describe app features that appear on the official App Store listing.
  • Note device and OS limitations, and show the date each guide was last reviewed.

If a step is out of date or something is wrong, please tell us and we'll correct it.
