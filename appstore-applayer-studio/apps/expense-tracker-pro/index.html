Overview

Expense Tracker Pro is designed for quick daily logging. A numeric keypad gets each expense recorded in seconds, sorted into your own categories.

Set monthly budgets and watch progress in real time, review trends with visual analytics, and search or edit your full history. Expenses can be exported to CSV at any time.

Inside the app and in parts of its listing, it is called Daily Expense Tracker.

Key features

  • One-tap logging

    A fast numeric keypad for entering expenses.

  • Custom categories

    Organize spending your way.

  • Monthly budgets

    Track progress against your budget in real time.

  • Visual analytics

    See spending trends at a glance.

  • History and search

    A searchable, editable record of every expense.

  • Multi-currency and CSV export

    Track in different currencies and export anytime.

  • Siri Shortcuts

    Log expenses by voice.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Tracking daily spending
  • Saving toward a goal with a monthly budget
  • Exporting expenses to a spreadsheet
