AI Cleaner: Free Up Storage+

Find duplicate photos, large videos and messy contacts.

Scans your photo library for duplicates, similar shots, blurry pictures and screenshots, finds your largest videos, and merges duplicate contacts. You review everything before it is deleted.

Overview

AI Cleaner (Cleaner Guru inside the app) groups photos into duplicates, similar shots, blurry or low-quality pictures and screenshots, so you can keep the best and remove the rest in bulk.

It also sorts videos and large files by size and cleans up contacts by merging duplicates and flagging incomplete entries.

According to the listing, scanning happens on your device, and nothing is removed without your confirmation.

Inside the app and in parts of its listing, it is called Cleaner Guru.

Key features

  • Photo cleaner

    Groups duplicates, similar shots, blurry pictures and screenshots.

  • Video and large-file cleanup

    Sorts your biggest videos by size.

  • Contacts cleaner

    Merges duplicates and finds incomplete entries.

  • Review before deleting

    Nothing is removed without your confirmation.

  • On-device scanning

    Photos and data stay on your phone.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Clearing a "Storage Almost Full" warning
  • Removing burst shots and near-duplicates
  • Tidying a messy address book

Questions

Where do deleted photos go?

On iPhone, photos you delete usually move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos for about 30 days before they are permanently removed, so you can recover mistakes.
