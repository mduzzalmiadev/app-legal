PDF Scanner Pro: Document Scan

Scan, OCR, sign and organize documents.

Scan documents with automatic edge detection, turn photos into PDFs, extract text with OCR, add signatures, and keep everything organized in folders.

iPhone PDF & Documents
View on the App Store Free download. Offers in-app purchases.

Screenshots

Screenshots from the official App Store listing.

Overview

PDF Scanner Pro uses your iPhone camera to capture documents, receipts, forms, business cards, books and whiteboards. It detects edges, crops and corrects perspective automatically.

Scans become PDFs you can merge, reorder and rotate. OCR extracts editable text you can copy, search and share, and the listing says it supports multiple languages.

You can add signatures and annotations, fill out forms, organize files into folders, and export as PDF or image, or print directly.

Key features

  • Smart document scanner

    Auto edge detection, smart crop and perspective correction for single or multi-page scans.

  • Photo to PDF

    Convert photos to PDF, then merge, reorder, rotate and organize pages.

  • OCR text recognition

    Extract editable text from scans and copy, search or share it.

  • Sign and annotate

    Add signatures, notes and annotations, and fill out forms.

  • Enhance

    Color, grayscale, black-and-white or original filters, plus brightness, contrast and rotation.

  • Organize

    Folders, renaming and instant search.

  • Export and print

    Share as PDF or image via email, messaging or cloud storage, or print from your device.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Digitizing receipts, invoices and contracts
  • Scanning class notes and book pages
  • Signing and returning a form without a printer
  • Turning paperwork into searchable PDFs

Questions

Does scanning work offline?

The listing lists "works offline for scanning" among its features.

Is it free?

PDF Scanner Pro is free to download. Its listing says unlimited scanning, OCR, editing and premium features require a subscription, with a free trial on the weekly plan.
