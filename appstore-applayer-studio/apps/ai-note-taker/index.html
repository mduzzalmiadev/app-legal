AI Note Taker: Voice to Text.

Record and transcribe on-device, in real time.

Records meetings, lectures and notes and transcribes them live on your device in 11 languages, with optional AI writing tools for summaries and rewrites.

Overview

AI Note Taker records audio and transcribes speech as you talk. The listing states that transcription runs fully offline on your device, so audio and transcripts don't have to leave your phone.

Recordings can be sorted into folders such as Journal, Study, Travel and Ideas, and transcripts can be exported as TXT, PDF or Word-compatible DOCX.

Optional AI writing tools, which require an internet connection, can summarize a transcript, fix grammar, change tone, translate, or turn it into bullet points, action items or an email draft.

Inside the app and in parts of its listing, it is called Voice to Text.

Key features

  • Live on-device transcription

    Real-time speech-to-text that works offline.

  • 11 transcription languages

    English (US/UK), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (BR), Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Bengali.

  • Record and play back

    One-tap recording with a level meter, pause/resume and scrubbing.

  • Folders and tags

    Organize recordings by category and filter in one tap.

  • AI writing tools

    Summaries, grammar fixes, tone changes, translation, action items and drafts (internet required, Pro).

  • Export

    Share transcripts as TXT, PDF or DOCX.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Transcribing lectures and study sessions
  • Capturing meeting notes and action items
  • Dictating ideas and journal entries

Questions

Does transcription need the internet?

No. The listing says transcription runs fully offline on-device. Only the AI writing tools need an internet connection.

What is free?

Recording, transcribing and organizing are free. Voice to Text Pro, a weekly, monthly, yearly or lifetime plan, unlocks the AI writing tools.
