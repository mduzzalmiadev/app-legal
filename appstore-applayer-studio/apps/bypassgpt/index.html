BypassGPT: AI Humanizer

Rewrite, check, chat and translate in one app.

Overview

BypassGPT has four writing tools. Humanize rewrites pasted text in a Natural, Professional, Friendly or Academic tone. Detect gives an AI-vs-human probability score with a writing analysis and suggestions.

Translate covers 48 languages with automatic language detection, and AI Writing Chat is a streaming assistant for brainstorming and drafting.

Results from every tool are saved to a searchable history, and favorites keep your best outputs close.

Key features

  • Humanize

    Rewrite text in Natural, Professional, Friendly or Academic tone.

  • AI detection

    AI-vs-human probability score, writing analysis and suggestions.

  • Translate

    48 languages with auto-detection and one-tap swapping.

  • AI writing chat

    Streaming chat with multiple saved conversations.

  • History and favorites

    Searchable history across all tools.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Making a stiff draft email read more naturally
  • Translating a message into another language
  • Brainstorming outlines and first drafts

Questions

Is the AI detection score definitive?

No. AI detection tools give probability estimates and can be wrong. Treat the score as a writing aid, and follow your school's or employer's rules on AI use.
