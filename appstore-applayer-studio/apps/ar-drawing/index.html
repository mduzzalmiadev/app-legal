AR Drawing: Draw on Paper

Trace any sketch onto real paper with your camera.

Project a sketch through your camera and trace it onto paper, use the screen as a light box, or paint digitally, with hundreds of templates and photo-to-sketch.

Overview

AR Drawing helps you learn to draw by tracing. In Camera mode you place paper on a table, point your phone at it, and trace the projected image, with adjustable opacity, positioning, mirroring and screen lock.

Screen mode turns the phone into a light source you can trace on directly, and Painting mode is a full digital canvas with brushes, fill, stickers and 30 steps of undo.

You can import your own photos and, with Pro, convert them to pencil sketches in 20 styles. Drawing sessions can be recorded as video.

Key features

  • Camera mode

    Trace a projected image onto paper with opacity, scale, rotation, mirroring, flash and screen lock.

  • Screen mode

    Use the screen as a light box with pinch, rotate and pan.

  • Painting mode

    Brush, eraser, fill, custom colors, stickers and 30-step undo/redo.

  • Drawing library

    Hundreds of templates: anime, kawaii, animals, cars, nature, people and more.

  • AI image to sketch

    Turn a photo into a pencil sketch in 20 styles (Pro).

  • Text styles

    100+ device fonts with stroke and fill styling.

  • Record and share

    Record your drawing session as a video.

  • Offline templates

    Templates work offline and previously loaded images are cached.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Learning to draw by tracing
  • Copying a photo outline onto paper or canvas
  • Recording a time-lapse-style drawing video
