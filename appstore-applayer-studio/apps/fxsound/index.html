FxSound - Volume Boost & EQ

Louder volume, deeper bass and a 5-band EQ.

An offline music player with a volume booster, bass booster, 5-band equalizer with presets, and an A/B comparison against the original sound.

iPhone Audio & Music
View on the App Store Free download. Offers in-app purchases.

Screenshots

Screenshots from the official App Store listing.

Overview

Import your songs and videos, then shape the sound with bass boost, volume boost and a 5-band equalizer with genre presets.

A Boosted vs Original toggle lets you A/B compare your settings against the unprocessed audio. Playback works offline once your files are imported.

Inside the app and in parts of its listing, it is called Bass Booster.

Key features

  • Volume booster

    Boost playback loudness of imported audio.

  • Bass booster

    Add low-end with a single control.

  • 5-band equalizer

    Adjust highs, mids and lows, with ready-made presets.

  • Boosted vs Original

    A/B compare enhanced audio against the original.

  • Offline player

    Play imported music and videos without an internet connection.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Getting more bass from headphones, earbuds or car audio
  • Tuning the EQ for a particular genre
  • Listening to an imported music library offline

Questions

Does it boost the volume of other apps?

The listing describes boosting audio you import into the app's own player. It does not claim to change system-wide volume.

Can loud volume damage hearing?

Yes. Listening at high volume for long periods can harm hearing. Keep boosted volume at a comfortable level.
