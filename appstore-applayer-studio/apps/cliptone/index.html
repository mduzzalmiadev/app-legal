ClipTone: Ringtone Maker

Turn any video or audio clip into a custom tone.

Trim a favorite moment from a video or audio file and save it as a ringtone, alarm sound, text tone or charging sound.

Overview

ClipTone is a ringtone maker for iPhone. Pick a video or an audio file, trim the part you want with a waveform editor, preview it, and save it as a tone.

Because iOS does not let third-party apps change your ringtone directly, ClipTone sets ringtones and alert tones through Apple's free GarageBand app, as stated on the App Store listing.

It also keeps a collection of everything you create (ringtones, alarms, text tones and charging sounds), organized by category, with favorites.

Key features

  • Extract audio from video

    Use the sound from any video in your library, or import audio files directly.

  • Waveform trimming

    Select precise start and end points on a visual waveform and preview before saving.

  • Ringtones and alert tones

    Set as a ringtone or alert tone via GarageBand.

  • Alarm sounds

    Create custom alarm sounds from any audio.

  • Text tones

    Make unique notification sounds for messages.

  • Charging sounds

    Set custom sounds for charge start, charge end, full charge and low battery.

  • My Collection

    Rename, organize and favorite your tones by category.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Making a ringtone from a song clip or a video you recorded
  • Giving a contact or messages a distinctive tone
  • Waking up to a favorite sound
  • Personalizing iPhone charging sounds

Questions

Why does ClipTone use GarageBand?

iOS only lets apps add ringtones through Apple's own channels. ClipTone's listing states that ringtones and alert tones are set via GarageBand, Apple's free music app. Our ringtone guide walks through the full process.

Is ClipTone free?

ClipTone is free to download. Its listing says all features are unlocked with an auto-renewing subscription that includes a 3-day free trial. Prices vary by location and are shown in the app.
