Invoice Maker Pro: Invy

Professional invoices and estimates from your iPhone.

Create invoices and estimates with live totals, 20 PDF templates, client and product libraries, a revenue dashboard and Siri support. Data is stored on your device.

Overview

Invoice Maker Pro is built for freelancers and small businesses. Add line items with quantity, price, tax and discounts, and totals update instantly. Estimates convert to invoices with one tap.

Choose from 20 PDF templates, add your logo and signature, then share by email, Messages or AirDrop, or print. Every document is tracked as Draft, Unpaid, Paid or Overdue.

A dashboard shows revenue, outstanding balances and a 6-month trend. The listing says the app works fully offline, with data stored on your device and backup/restore available.

Key features

  • Invoices and estimates

    Live totals with tax and discounts, auto-numbering, duplication, and one-tap estimate-to-invoice conversion.

  • 20 PDF templates

    Live template preview, then share as PDF or print.

  • Clients and products

    Save client profiles, import from Contacts, and keep a reusable product/service library with custom fields.

  • Status tracking

    Draft, Unpaid, Paid and Overdue.

  • Dashboard

    Revenue, outstanding balances, a 6-month trend chart and status breakdown.

  • Branding

    Logo, digital signature, default tax rates and 150+ currencies.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Create invoices by voice and automate with Apple Shortcuts.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Billing a client right after a job
  • Sending an estimate and converting it once approved
  • Keeping track of which invoices are overdue

Questions

Where is my data stored?

The listing says everything is stored on your device and the app works fully offline, with backup and restore available.

What does the free version include?

The free version includes the core features, with limits on the number of invoices, clients and products. Pro, a weekly or monthly subscription, removes the limits and unlocks all 20 templates.
