Scanzo: QR Scanner & Generator

Scan, create and style QR codes and barcodes.

Fast QR and barcode scanning with smart actions and history, plus a generator for links, Wi-Fi, contacts and barcodes with custom colors and patterns.

Overview

Scanzo detects QR codes and barcodes as soon as they enter the viewfinder, with a torch for low light, and it can also scan codes from photos.

It understands what you scanned. Links open in an in-app browser, Wi-Fi codes let you copy the password, contacts save to your address book, and phone, SMS, email and location codes open the right app.

You can create QR codes for text, links, Wi-Fi networks and contacts, and EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and Code 128 barcodes, styled with your own colors, themes and patterns. Scan history stays on your device.

Inside the app and in parts of its listing, it is called QR Scan.

Key features

  • Instant scanning

    QR codes and barcodes from the camera or your photo library, with a torch.

  • Smart actions

    Open links, copy Wi-Fi passwords, save contacts, call, text, email or open Maps.

  • Scan history

    Saved automatically on your device; clear it anytime.

  • QR generator

    Text, links, Wi-Fi and vCard contacts.

  • Barcode generator

    EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and Code 128.

  • Custom styles

    Colors, themes and classic or rounded patterns with live preview.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Sharing your Wi-Fi with guests via a QR code
  • Scanning a QR code from a screenshot
  • Creating a branded QR code for a flyer
