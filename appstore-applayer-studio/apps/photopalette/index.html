Overview

PhotoPalette turns everyday snapshots into styled cards for your feed, lock screen, scrapbook or a private keepsake.

Pick from hand-picked palettes, subtle vintage filters, Polaroid frames and scrapbook templates, then add stickers, fonts and gradient backgrounds before exporting in high resolution.

Key features

  • Color palettes

    Hand-picked palettes inspired by film, nature and art.

  • Vintage filters

    Retro looks that stay subtle.

  • Polaroid frames

    Borders and instant-camera looks.

  • Scrapbook templates

    Memory pages and layouts in one tap.

  • Stickers, fonts and backgrounds

    Sticker packs, fonts and gradient backgrounds.

  • High-resolution export

    Share, print or save.
