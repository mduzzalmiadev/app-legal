Overview

3uTools Guide is an educational companion for iOS device management. Its tutorials are organized by skill level and topic: Beginner, Advanced, Backup & Restore, Flashing, Device Management and Troubleshooting.

A troubleshooting library explains common error codes such as 4013, 4014, 3194, Error 9 and Error 53, and a built-in AI assistant answers questions about backups, firmware and errors.

Per its listing, 3uTools Guide is an independent, unofficial educational guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with 3uTools or its developers.

Key features

  • Step-by-step guides

    Tutorials organized from beginner to advanced.

  • Error-code troubleshooting

  • Firmware and device references

Questions

Is this the official 3uTools app?

No. The App Store listing states it is an independent, unofficial educational guide, not affiliated with or endorsed by 3uTools or its developers.

Should I back up first?

Yes. Back up your device before any restore, update or firmware change. Apple's official guidance for backing up and restoring is at support.apple.com.
