AI Keyboard & Writer: TypeAI

A keyboard with snippets, OCR and built-in tools.

A custom keyboard for saving and pasting text snippets, scanning text from photos with on-device OCR, and using a calculator, password generator and converters without switching apps.

Overview

TypeAI (named AutoPaste inside the app) is a custom iPhone keyboard. Save the text you type repeatedly, such as addresses, templates and canned replies, then paste it into any app with a tap.

Its OCR page pulls text out of a photo using Apple's on-device Vision framework and inserts it into the current field. Built-in tools include a scientific and VAT calculator, password generator, date/time insertion, invoice numbers, unit and case converters.

The listing states that keystrokes are never sent anywhere, there are no accounts or cloud sync, and snippets are protected by Face ID / Touch ID. Full Access is required for pasting and OCR.

Inside the app and in parts of its listing, it is called AutoPaste.

Key features

  • Snippets

    Save and paste reusable text, organized into categories.

  • Scan text from photos

    On-device OCR inserts recognized text into the current field.

  • Calculators

    Scientific calculator plus a VAT calculator with presets for 10 European countries or a custom rate.

  • Power-user tools

    Password generator, date/time insert, auto-incrementing invoice numbers, unit and case converters.

  • QWERTY + emoji

    Full layout with word suggestions and a frequent-emoji bar.

  • Privacy

    No accounts or cloud sync; snippets protected by Face ID / Touch ID.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Replying with the same phrases in different apps
  • Typing an address or bank details without retyping
  • Copying text from a receipt or business card photo into a form

Questions

Why does the keyboard need Full Access?

iOS requires Full Access for a keyboard to paste snippets into other apps and to read photos you pick for OCR. The listing says the permission is used only for those actions.
