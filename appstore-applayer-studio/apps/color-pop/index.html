Color Pop: Coloring Book

Tap to fill, or paint with realistic brushes.

A relaxing coloring book with 1,700+ pages in 36 categories, tap-to-fill that stays inside the lines, 20+ realistic brushes and color inspiration.

Overview

Color Pop (shown as Magic Color Pop in the app) is a calm coloring studio. Tap any area and it fills inside the lines, or pick up a brush such as watercolor, oil, pencil, glitter or neon and paint your own way.

Inspiration shows a page fully colored in themes like Sunny, Pastel and Forest, and Magic tap fills each area with its suggested color. There is a new Art of the Day every day, and you can turn your own photos into coloring pages.

Finished art saves to Photos or exports in HD as PNG, JPG, HEIF or PDF.

Inside the app and in parts of its listing, it is called Magic Color Pop.

Key features

  • Tap to fill

    One tap fills an area perfectly inside the lines.

  • Inspiration and Magic tap

    Preview color themes, then fill with suggested colors.

  • 20+ realistic brushes

    Bristle, pencils, watercolor, oil, splatter, glitter, neon and more.

  • 1,700+ pages

    36 categories including animals, mandalas, flowers and seasonal themes.

  • Art of the Day

    A new page every day, free to color.

  • Photo to coloring page

    Turn your own photos into pages (Premium).

  • HD export

    PNG, JPG, HEIF or PDF.

Features as described on the App Store listing. Some require an in-app purchase.

Good for

  • Unwinding for a few minutes
  • Coloring mandalas and detailed pages with zoom
  • Turning a family photo into a coloring page

Questions

What is free?

The listing says today's Art of the Day is free to color. Premium, a weekly subscription or one-time lifetime purchase, unlocks every page, Pro brushes, photo-to-coloring-page and unlimited HD exports.
