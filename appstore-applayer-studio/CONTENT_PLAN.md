# Content plan

## Editorial rules

1. **One real question per guide.** Pick a task a person types into Google ("how to … on iPhone"), not a keyword list.
2. **Answer first.** The opening paragraph gives the direct answer, then the steps.
3. **Free option first.** If Apple's built-in apps can do it, show that. Recommend our app only where it genuinely saves time or does more, and say why.
4. **Only listed features.** Describe an app's features exactly as its App Store listing does. Never imply ours is the only solution.
5. **Test on a device.** Follow every step on current iOS/macOS before publishing, then fill in the "Last reviewed" date and the OS versions you checked in the byline.
6. **Caveats are content.** Device requirements, OS versions, DRM and copyright limits, and subscription requirements all belong in the guide.
7. **Link deliberately.** Every guide links to 2–3 related guides and to the app pages it mentions. No doorway pages or thin variations ("…on iPhone 15", "…on iPhone 16").
8. **Refresh, don't duplicate.** When iOS changes a menu, update the existing guide and its "Last reviewed" date.

## Cadence

Two well-tested guides a month is more valuable than ten untested ones. After about three months, review Search Console: update guides with impressions but low clicks (improve titles and descriptions), and expand guides that rank on page 2.
