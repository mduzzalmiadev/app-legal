Privacy Policy

AI Note Taker (App Store name: “AI Note Taker Pro: Summary”)
Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the AI Note Taker app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, how it is collected, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The App is developed by MD UZZAL MIA.

In short
  • Your audio recordings and transcripts are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not upload your recordings.
  • Only if you use an AI feature, and only after you give permission in the App, the transcript text of that recording is sent to OpenAI to generate the result.
  • We do not use advertising, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not collect your location, contacts or photos.
  • We never sell your data.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

1.1 Audio recordings

When you grant microphone access, the App records audio that you start yourself. Recordings are saved in the App’s private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to any AI service. Audio files leave your device only if you choose to export or share them (for example via the iOS share sheet), or as part of your own device backups (iCloud or computer backup), which are controlled by Apple and by you.

1.2 Speech-to-text transcription

Transcription uses Apple’s Speech Recognition framework. For languages that support on-device recognition, transcription runs entirely on your device. For languages that do not support it, Apple’s Speech Recognition may send the audio to Apple’s servers to produce the transcript. That processing is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive the audio. Transcripts are saved on your device.

1.3 AI features (shared with OpenAI)

The App offers optional AI features: summaries, mind maps, rewriting, translation, and the AI Assistant chat.

  • What is sent: the transcript text of the recording you selected, the instruction for the feature you chose (for example “Summarize”), and, in the AI Assistant, your typed messages and recent chat messages for that recording. Audio is never sent to OpenAI. No name, email, contact details, or device identifiers are sent.
  • Who it is sent to: OpenAI, L.L.C. (“OpenAI”), through the OpenAI API.
  • Why: only to generate the result you asked for. The result is shown to you and saved on your device.
  • Your permission: before the first AI request, the App shows a notice explaining this and asks for your permission. Nothing is sent unless you tap “Continue”. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → AI Data Processing. After that, the App asks again before sending anything.
  • How OpenAI handles it: under OpenAI’s API data policies, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI’s models by default and may be kept by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse and misuse monitoring before deletion. OpenAI processes this data under contractual obligations that give protection equal to or greater than this Policy. See the OpenAI Privacy Policy and OpenAI API data privacy.

1.4 Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you have an active subscription and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (products bought, dates, renewal status), an anonymous app user ID created by the App, and a device identifier (the iOS identifier for vendor). We use this to unlock premium features and to see aggregated subscription statistics. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

1.5 App configuration

We use Firebase Remote Config, provided by Google LLC, to load App settings such as which AI model to use. To do this, Firebase uses a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this installation of the App). It is used only to deliver configuration, not for advertising or analytics. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

1.6 Settings stored on your device

Preferences such as language, theme, and your AI permission choice are stored only on your device.

1.7 Support emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you.

3. What we do not collect

  • No advertising or advertising identifiers (IDFA), and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health data.
  • No account or sign-up is required.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Recordings, transcripts, AI results and chat history stay on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Data sent to OpenAI is kept by OpenAI according to its API policies (generally up to 30 days, then deleted).
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase records as needed to provide subscription services and meet legal obligations. To request deletion of RevenueCat data linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the email below.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can deny or revoke microphone and speech recognition access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • You can turn off sharing with OpenAI in the App under Settings → AI Data Processing. The App keeps working without AI features.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Because recordings and transcripts are stored only on your device, you control them directly. For any other request, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Data sent to OpenAI, RevenueCat and Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Data on your device is protected by iOS security and data protection.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we may also let you know in the App.
