Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BypassGPT ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the app. BypassGPT is designed to store as little as possible: there is no account system, no login, and no analytics or advertising tracking. This policy describes exactly what is processed and by whom.

1. Information We Collect

BypassGPT does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier to use the app.

The App processes the following, only when you actively use a feature:

  • Text you enter or import — content you type, paste, dictate, or extract from a photo or PDF, in order to use the Humanize, AI Detector, Translator, or AI Chat features.
  • Voice input — if you use dictation, audio is processed by Apple's on-device Speech Recognition to convert speech to text. Audio is not sent to us or stored by the App.
  • Photos and documents — if you use the camera or photo/PDF import to extract text, images are processed on-device using Apple's Vision framework (on-device text recognition). Images themselves are not uploaded or stored by the App beyond the extraction step.
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe to BypassGPT Premium, your purchase is processed by Apple and RevenueCat as described in Section 3.

2. How Your Text Is Processed

When you use Humanize, AI Detector, Translator, or AI Chat, the text you submit is sent directly from your device to OpenAI's API to generate a response. This is necessary for these features to work — there is no way to humanize, analyze, translate, or chat without sending the relevant text to the AI provider.

  • We do not attach your name, email, device ID, or any other identifier to these requests.
  • OpenAI processes this text according to its own privacy policy and API data-usage terms, which govern how long that data may be retained and how it may be used by OpenAI. We encourage you to review OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Do not submit sensitive personal information (e.g., health, financial, or government ID data) into any AI feature, since this data will be transmitted to a third-party AI provider.

3. Local Storage — What Stays on Your Device

Your history (past Humanize results, AI Detector checks, Translations, and Chat conversations) and Favorites are stored only on your device, using Apple's local on-device storage framework. This data is never uploaded to our servers and is not synced to iCloud.

If you delete the App, or clear an item from History or Favorites within the App, that data is permanently removed from your device. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it for you.

4. Subscriptions & Payment

BypassGPT offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly) and a Lifetime purchase, processed through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system. We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and validate purchases.

  • We never see or store your payment details (card number, Apple ID password, etc.) — these are handled entirely by Apple.
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous, app-generated identifier and transaction/purchase data (product purchased, price, renewal status) to determine whether you have an active subscription. It is not linked to your name or email by us.
  • See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for details on their handling of this data.

5. Analytics, Advertising & Tracking

BypassGPT does not use any analytics SDK, advertising network, crash-reporting tool, or cross-app/cross-site tracking technology. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and do not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because the App does not track you across apps or websites.

6. Device Permissions

  • Microphone / Speech Recognition — requested only if you tap the dictation button; used solely to convert your speech to text on-device.
  • Camera / Photo Library — requested only if you choose to scan or import a photo/PDF to extract text.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app. Declining them simply disables the related input method — all other features continue to work normally.

7. Data Retention

Text you submit to AI features passes through OpenAI's systems as described in Section 2 and is retained according to OpenAI's own policies, not ours. Locally stored History and Favorites remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.

8. Children's Privacy

BypassGPT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that concerns you, please contact us using the details below.

9. Your Rights

Because BypassGPT does not maintain user accounts or a server-side database of your personal information, most of your data lives only on your device, under your control (you can view, export via Share, or delete it at any time within the App). For information submitted to OpenAI or RevenueCat, please refer to their respective privacy policies for your rights regarding that data, or contact us and we will assist where we are able.

10. International Users

Your text may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence (for example, by OpenAI or RevenueCat's infrastructure). By using the App, you understand that your submitted text may be transferred to and processed in other countries, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, notify you within the App. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
