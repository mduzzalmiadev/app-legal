Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BypassGPT (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use the App on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. BypassGPT is designed to collect as little as possible: there is no account, no login, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking.

In short
  • Your text is sent to OpenAI only after you tap “Allow” on the AI data-sharing screen, and only when you use an AI feature.
  • Voice dictation, photo scanning, and PDF text extraction happen on your device.
  • Your History, Chats, and Favorites stay on your device.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no account.

1. Information We Collect

BypassGPT does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier. The App processes the following only when you actively use a feature:

  • Text you enter or import: content you type, paste, dictate, or extract from a photo or PDF, so you can use Humanize, AI Detector, Writing Tools, Translator, or AI Chat.
  • Voice input: when you use dictation, audio is converted to text by Apple’s Speech Recognition entirely on your device. Audio is never sent to Apple’s servers, to us, or to anyone else, and it is not stored. If your device has no on-device speech model for your language, dictation is unavailable rather than falling back to a server.
  • Photos and documents: when you scan with the camera or import a photo or PDF, text is extracted on your device using Apple’s Vision and PDFKit frameworks. The images and files themselves are not uploaded or kept by the App.
  • Purchase information: if you buy BypassGPT Premium, the purchase is handled by Apple and RevenueCat, as described in Section 4.

2. How Your Text Is Processed by AI

Humanize, AI Detector, Writing Tools (such as Fix Grammar, Paraphrase, and Summarize), Translator, and AI Chat are powered by OpenAI. To produce a result, the text you submit, and for AI Chat the recent conversation, is sent from your device to OpenAI’s API.

  • Your permission comes first. Before any text leaves your device, the App asks for your permission on an AI data-sharing screen. Nothing is sent until you tap “Allow.” You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → “Share Text with AI.” While permission is off, AI features will not send anything.
  • We do not attach your name, email, or any device identifier to these requests.
  • OpenAI processes this text under its own Privacy Policy and API data-usage terms. Those terms govern how long OpenAI may keep the data. Under OpenAI’s API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default.
  • Please do not submit sensitive information, such as passwords, health, financial, or government ID data, to any AI feature.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your History (past Humanize, AI Detector, Writing Tools, and Translator results), your Chat conversations, and your Favorites are stored only on your device, using Apple’s on-device storage. This data is not uploaded to us and is not synced to iCloud.

When you delete an item in the App, or delete the App itself, that data is permanently removed. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it.

The App also stores a few settings on your device, such as your theme, language, onboarding status, and AI data-sharing choice.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

BypassGPT offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly) and a one-time Lifetime purchase through Apple’s in-app purchase system. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active purchase.

  • We never see your payment details. These are handled entirely by Apple.
  • RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app-generated identifier and transaction data (the product, price, and renewal status). This is not linked to your name or email.
  • See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

5. App Configuration (Firebase Remote Config)

The App uses Google Firebase Remote Config only to load app configuration when it launches. To deliver this configuration, Firebase processes a random app-installation identifier and your IP address. This information is not used for analytics, advertising, or tracking. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Analytics, Advertising & Tracking

BypassGPT does not use any analytics SDK, advertising network, or crash-reporting tool, and it does not show ads. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or track you across other apps or websites, so the App does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

7. Device Permissions

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: requested only when you tap the dictation button, and used only to turn your speech into text on your device.
  • Camera: requested only when you choose to scan text.

Photos and files you import are chosen through Apple’s system pickers, which share only the item you select. You can grant or revoke permissions at any time in your device’s Settings. Declining a permission only disables that input method. Everything else keeps working.

8. Data Retention

Text you send to AI features is kept by OpenAI only as its own policies allow (see Section 2). We do not keep any copy on servers of our own. Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App.

9. Children’s Privacy

BypassGPT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that concerns you, please contact us.

10. Your Rights & Choices

We do not run user accounts or a server database of personal information, so your data stays on your device, under your control. You can view, share, or delete it at any time in the App, and you can turn off AI data sharing in Settings. For data held by OpenAI or RevenueCat, see their privacy policies. You can also contact us, and we will help where we can.

11. International Users

Text you send to AI features, and purchase data, may be processed on servers outside your country, for example by OpenAI, RevenueCat, or Google. Those countries may have different data-protection laws from yours.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the App.
