Zoom Camera

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zoom Camera (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad.

In short: your photos and videos stay on your device. We don’t run analytics or ads, and we don’t track you. The only third-party service the app uses is RevenueCat, which processes in-app purchases.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not upload, view or store your photos, videos or recordings on our servers.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.

2. Device permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when a feature needs them. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings.

PermissionWhy it is used
CameraTo show the live preview and take zoomed photos and videos.
MicrophoneTo record sound with your videos. If you decline, videos are recorded without sound.
Photos (add only)To save your captures to your Photos library when “Save Original Photos” is turned on. The app cannot read your existing library.

3. Photos, videos and History

Photos and videos you capture are stored locally on your device, inside the app’s private storage, and shown in History. They are not sent to us or to any third party. Deleting an item in History removes it from the app. Copies already saved to your Photos library are not affected. Deleting the app removes all of its stored captures.

4. Text scanning and translation

“Scan Distant Text” reads text from a camera frame using Apple’s on-device Vision framework. The frame is not saved and is not sent to us. If you choose Translate, the text is handled by Apple’s system Translation feature under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

Zoom Camera Pro purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never receive your payment details.

To check whether you have an active subscription and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID
  • your purchase and subscription history for this app
  • basic technical data, such as device model, iOS version, app version, country/region and IP address

This data is used only to provide and restore your purchases. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy for details.

6. Settings stored on your device

Your preferences, such as default mode, grid, level, haptics, video quality and appearance, along with whether you have finished onboarding, are stored only on your device.

7. App Store ratings

The app may show Apple’s standard rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt and any rating or review you submit. We do not receive any personal information from it.

8. Children’s privacy

Zoom Camera is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and deletion

We keep no personal data on our own servers. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your purchases and to meet legal requirements. To ask for deletion of purchase data linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the address below.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU/UK or California, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold almost no personal data, most requests are handled by deleting the app or managing iOS permissions. For anything else, contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
