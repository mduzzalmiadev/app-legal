Privacy Policy

Audio Editor · Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Audio Editor ("Audio Editor," "the App," "we," "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

Your audio files and projects stay on your device. We do not upload, store, or have access to the audio you record, import, or edit. The only data that leaves your device relates to subscription purchases, handled by Apple and our payment/subscription provider, RevenueCat.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App has no account system, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. We do not collect your name, contact list, photos, or location. We do not run advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Audio, Projects & Files

Recordings, imported files, exported files, projects, and waveform data are stored locally in the App's private storage area on your device. They are not transmitted to us or to any third party by the App.

If you use the Files app, AirDrop, or a share sheet to export or send a file, that transfer is initiated and controlled by you through Apple's system frameworks.

4. Microphone Access

The App requests microphone access only when you tap Record, allowing you to capture audio directly into a project.

Microphone access is not requested at launch or during onboarding. Recorded audio is processed and stored only on your device.

You can review or revoke this permission anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

5. Subscriptions & Purchases

The App offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions and/or lifetime purchases through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes all payments, and we do not receive or store your payment card details.

To manage subscription entitlements and purchases, the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service.

RevenueCat may receive:

  • An anonymous app user identifier
  • Purchase and transaction information
  • Product and subscription status
  • Basic device and app information required for subscription management

RevenueCat does not receive your audio recordings or project files from the App.

For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy .

6. Diagnostic Data

The App does not operate its own third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

If you voluntarily share diagnostics through Apple's system tools, Apple may provide diagnostic information to developers according to Apple's policies and your device settings.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your audio recordings and project files are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App removes locally stored App data, subject to Apple's normal device backup behavior.

For requests concerning subscription-related information handled by RevenueCat, contact us using the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Local files are protected by Apple's standard app sandbox and device security mechanisms. Information transmitted to Apple or RevenueCat for purchase and subscription management is transmitted using encrypted connections.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal information associated with your purchases or use of the App.

Because the App does not maintain a user account or cloud storage system, most audio and project data remains under your control on your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Email us at mduzzalmia.dev@gmail.com .
