Privacy Policy

Audio Recorder · Last updated: September 19, 2026

Independent & unofficial app. Audio Recorder is an independent app made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Audacity®, the Audacity audio editor, the Audacity project, or Muse Group. Audacity is a trademark of its respective owner and is mentioned only to make clear that this app is a different, unrelated product.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Audio Recorder (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles when you use it. In short: your recordings stay on your device, and we do not run our own servers that store your audio or transcripts.

1. Information the App handles

  • Your recordings and transcripts. Audio you record, files you edit, merge or convert, and any transcripts are stored locally in the App’s storage on your device. We cannot access them, and they are not uploaded to us. They may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup) according to your device settings.
  • Microphone. The App uses the microphone only to record audio when you choose to record. It does not record in the background on its own.
  • Speech recognition (transcription). When you use Transcribe, your recording is converted to text by Apple’s Speech Recognition. This runs on your device when your language supports it. Otherwise the audio may be sent to Apple to be processed, subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. The App asks for your permission before any transcription starts.
  • AI tools. If you choose an AI tool (Summarize, Fix Grammar, Bullet Points or Action Items), the transcript text (not the audio) is sent to OpenAI’s GPT-5 model to generate the result, subject to OpenAI’s Privacy Policy. Nothing is sent to OpenAI unless you choose one of these tools, and only after you have agreed in the App.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Payments are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage your Pro subscription status; this involves an anonymous app user identifier and purchase receipt information.
  • App configuration and analytics. We use Google Firebase (Remote Config and Analytics) to deliver app settings and to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used and whether it works well. This can include device and app information such as app version, device model, operating system and an app-instance identifier. It does not include the content of your recordings or transcripts.
  • Support messages. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the message you send. The pre-filled email may include your app version, device model and iOS version to help us fix problems.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App’s features: recording, editing, merging, converting and transcribing audio.
  • To process purchases and unlock Pro features.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the App, and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your recordings or transcripts for advertising.

4. Storage, retention and deletion

Recordings and transcripts remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Deleting a recording removes its audio file from the App. Information held by the third-party services above is kept according to their own retention policies.

5. Your choices

  • Permissions. You can turn microphone and speech recognition access on or off at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • Speech & AI consent. You can withdraw your agreement to speech and AI processing in the App under Settings > Speech & AI Processing.
  • Subscriptions. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID account settings.
  • Analytics. To limit analytics, you can disable “Allow Apps to Request to Track” and related privacy options in iOS Settings, or contact us.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Your recordings are stored in the App’s protected storage on your device. Data sent to third-party services is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, they apply safeguards for such transfers under their own policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because your recordings are stored on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the App. For anything else, contact us at the address below.
