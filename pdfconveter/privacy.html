Privacy Policy

App: PDF Editor  ·  Effective date: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Editor ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad. We built PDF Editor to do its work on your device — most of what the app touches (your PDF files, edits, images) never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it yourself.

1. Summary

  • Your PDF documents, edits, and images are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or views your files.
  • The App requests Camera and Photo Library access only to let you add photos to a PDF — nothing is uploaded anywhere as a result.
  • If you purchase a subscription, purchase and entitlement information is handled by Apple and our subscription platform, RevenueCat, as described below.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Files and content you create in the App

PDF Editor lets you open, edit, annotate, merge, convert, and export PDF documents, and insert images or camera photos into them. All of this processing happens on your device using Apple's PDFKit and related frameworks. Your documents are stored in the App's local document storage (and, if you use the Share Extension, in a shared App Group container used only to hand files off between the Share Extension and the main app on your own device). We do not transmit your document content to our own servers, and we do not have access to it.

2.2 Camera and Photo Library

With your permission, the App accesses:

  • Camera — so you can take a photo and insert it directly into a PDF.
  • Photo Library (read) — so you can insert existing photos into a PDF or import images to convert into a PDF.
  • Photo Library (add) — so you can save exported images or converted files back to your photo library.

These permissions are used solely to perform the action you requested. Photos and camera captures are not collected, transmitted, or stored by us outside your device.

2.3 Subscription and purchase information

PDF Editor offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions, processed by Apple's App Store In-App Purchase system. To manage entitlements (e.g., confirming which features you've unlocked) we use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. When you subscribe, RevenueCat and Apple process:

  • An anonymous app-specific user identifier (not your name or Apple ID)
  • Purchase/transaction and subscription status data (product purchased, renewal date, active/expired status)
  • Device and app metadata needed to validate purchases (e.g., app version, device type, country/locale)

We never receive or see your payment card, bank, or Apple ID account details — those are handled entirely by Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy and Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

2.4 Information we do not collect

  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not require account creation, and we do not collect your name, email, or contact list.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your documents and images are stored in the App's local storage on your device (and in iCloud only if you separately enable iCloud Drive / device backups through iOS itself — a setting controlled by you, not by the App). We rely on Apple's standard iOS sandboxing and file-protection mechanisms to keep this data secure. Because we do not operate a backend server for your file content, we do not store copies of your documents.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data only with:

  • Apple — for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, and any iCloud sync you personally enable at the OS level.
  • RevenueCat — to manage and validate subscription entitlements, as described in Section 2.3.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our legal rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

6. Your Choices

  • You can grant or revoke Camera and Photo Library access at any time in Settings → Privacy → Camera / Photos → PDF Editor.
  • You can delete documents at any time from within the App; deleting the App removes its local storage and App Group data from your device (subject to normal iOS behavior for backups you've made).
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription any time via Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device, per Apple's standard subscription management.

7. Children's Privacy

PDF Editor is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data we hold about you. Because PDF Editor stores your document content only on your device and we hold minimal data about you (limited to what RevenueCat/Apple process for subscriptions, per Section 2.3), most such requests can be satisfied by managing the App and your subscription directly on your device. For anything else, contact us at the email below.

9. International Users

If you use the App outside the country in which our subscription/service providers operate, be aware that limited data described in Section 2.3 (purchase/entitlement data) may be processed in other countries, including the United States, by Apple and RevenueCat.
